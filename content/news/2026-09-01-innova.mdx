<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
We are celebrating the 2025 SusChem Award in the INNOVA category 🏆.
</p>
<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
This recognition acknowledges the work on biofuels carried out through the collaboration of US-ICMS, UNIZAR, and URBASER. 👨🔬 This research initiative has led to the development and patenting of innovative catalytic technology for producing biofuels for heavy-duty transport. The catalysts developed offer the potential to lower processing costs and provide flexibility regarding the final product.
 </p>   
<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
Congratulations, Tomas! 👏
</p>
