<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
Researchers and students from different research initiatives at the [University of Seville](https://www.us.es/) recently came together for a scientific exchange meeting, bringing together members of the [CLEVER-FUEL](https://clever-fuel-lab.eu/) and [CIRCULAR FUELS](https://circular-fuels.eu/) projects, alongside other students and early-career researchers.
</p>

<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
The meeting provided an opportunity to share ongoing research activities, discuss results and exchange ideas, identifying common interests and potential synergies across different research lines. It also highlighted the value of sharing scientific expertise, experience and experimental resources.
</p>
<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
For CLEVER-FUEL, these interactions contribute to fostering a dynamic and collaborative research environment, encouraging connections between researchers at different career stages and extending scientific exchange beyond individual projects.
</p>
<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
The meeting also reflected the importance of international research networks. Through its links with CIRCULAR FUELS, CLEVER-FUEL benefits from a broader collaborative environment connecting researchers and institutions, including the University of Hohenheim (Germany).
</p>
<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
By bringing together different research teams and perspectives, these activities help create opportunities for future collaboration and strengthen the scientific environment around CLEVER FUELS.
</p>
