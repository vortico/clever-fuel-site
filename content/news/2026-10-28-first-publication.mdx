<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
CLEVER-FUEL is up and running, and the first results are starting to come in! 🎉
</p>
<p style={{ marginBottom: '1.5em' }}>
Our team has just published the project's first scientific paper, exploring how the choice of catalyst support influences hydrogen-free hydrodeoxygenation, which is a promising approach to upgrading biomass-derived compounds for biofuel production.

This is an exciting first step, with plenty more research to come!
</p>
📄 **Read the paper:** [*Catalysts Design for Biofuel Production through a CLEVER-FUEL approach: Impact of support selection on Hydrogen-Free HDO*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scca.2026.100246)
